Summarize this synthetic lab in your [private workbook](../../worksheets/en/workbook.md):

1. Verify the actual Sol deployment/alias, GPT-5.5 judge, API, and exact agent version.
2. Compare answers with the synthetic policy sources and real tool results.
3. Distinguish unperformed work, request errors, missing rows, and quality failures; preserve raw evidence.
4. Use new labels for another comparison and change one condition, such as instructions. Record language, models, corpus, retrieval, and judge.
5. Never reuse previously seen holdout questions as a new unseen test.
6. Disable schedules, stop unnecessary sessions, and record retained resources, costs, and the next review date.
